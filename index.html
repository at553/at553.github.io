About Research

About

Research, in public.

I'm Avi, an AI researcher at PayPal. I work on post-training methods for conversational agents, collaborative AI systems, and foundation-model research for financial applications.

This site is a collection of notes, questions, and thoughts I find interesting. Outside of work, I enjoy reading philosophy and psychology to help me understand the world. In the spirit of Montaigne's Essays, I intend to use this space to follow ideas as they develop, revise them in public, and see what useful connections emerge therefrom. My hope is that intentionally committing ideas to writing will help me better organize and think critically about them (Quitadamo and Kurtz, 2007).
