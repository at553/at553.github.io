Focus

From models to systems.

My work sits at the boundary of machine learning, product engineering, and analytics: translating ambiguous business or research problems into measurable systems that can be evaluated, maintained, and improved.

Research and engineering themes
  • Evaluation and training infrastructure for multi-turn, tool-using AI agents.
  • Efficiency-aware reasoning methods for production latency and cost constraints.
  • Prescriptive analytics and optimization for decision-making systems.
  • Readable, maintainable software that keeps experimental work reproducible.
